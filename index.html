Longhorn Racing FSAE · 2025–present · Vehicle dynamics

Completed

Rear Upright

SolidWorks Ansys FEA nTOP DFM / DFA

Hover a bold term to change the drawing

The rear upright carries everything the tire does into the chassis. I ran the FEA on the assembly to find where stress was concentrating and where material was doing nothing.

Applying DFM and DFA to the rework kept it machinable for our shop, and the revision came out 5% lighter at a 1.75 safety factor.

Alongside it I designed the brake line routing and mounting hardware, and I am now writing test plans and generative design studies for additive-manufactured titanium parts driven by measured load cases.

A&M Robotics Automation & Design Lab · Summer 2026

Completed

REV GSE Cart

SolidWorks Sheet metal Raspberry Pi FDM / SLA

Hover a bold term to change the drawing

A troubleshooting workstation for a rover, built in the NASA ground-support equipment idiom: everything the team needs at the vehicle, rolled out over gravel and readable in sunlight. The riveted 5052-H32 frame came out of structural analysis on rivet loading and gusset design.

It carries telemetry and status LEDs driven by a Raspberry Pi, on gravel-terrain casters so it can follow the rover rather than wait in the lab.

I also built an AI tool-scanning system that traces tool profiles and generates custom foam-insert layouts, and managed sourcing for the 100+ item build.
